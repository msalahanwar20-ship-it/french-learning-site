Proven Study Blueprints

Master cognitive frameworks used by polyglots to absorb French fast.

1

Spaced Repetition (SRS)

Review vocabulary and rules just before your brain is about to forget them. Use apps like Anki or our built-in flashcard schedule intervals (1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days, 30 days).

  • Fight the forgetting curve
  • Move data to long-term memory
2

Active Recall & Output

Instead of passively rereading rules, force your brain to retrieve answers. Write mini-journals in French every evening, even if grammar is imperfect.

  • Self-testing over rereading
  • Shadowing native podcasts
3

Contextual Chunking

Never memorize isolated words. Learn phrases and collocations in context (e.g., instead of memorizing "besoin", learn "avoir besoin de").

  • Natural sentence flow
  • Faster speaking fluency
